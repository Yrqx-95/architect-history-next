## 1. Church of the Light

Architect: Tadao Ando
Current page slug: `church-of-light`

### Exam Connection

**1. Architect exam knowledge**

This case helps students understand daylighting, wall openings, interior orientation, material expression, and the relationship between simple form and spatial effect. It is useful when reviewing how light, enclosure, and section influence the quality of an interior space.

**2. Design studio knowledge**

Church of the Light is a compact lesson in editing. The building shows that a design can become stronger by reducing elements, controlling one dominant event, and making every surface support that event.

**3. Architectural history knowledge**

The building connects modern concrete architecture, Japanese spatial restraint, and late twentieth-century sacred space. It helps students see that modern religious architecture does not have to rely on historical ornament to produce symbolic meaning.

**4. What students often overlook**

Students often remember only the cross of light. They may miss the surrounding discipline: the darkness of the room, the thickness of the wall, the seating direction, and the silence created by exposed concrete.

**5. Further learning route**

Study daylighting and wall openings first, then compare other sacred spaces such as Chapel of Notre Dame du Haut, MIT Chapel, and Water Temple. After that, read more Tadao Ando works to see how concrete, path, and light repeat across different programs.

## 2. Villa Savoye

Architect: Le Corbusier
Current page slug: `villa-savoye`

### Exam Connection

**1. Architect exam knowledge**

This case helps students connect plan organization, structural grid, pilotis, roof garden, horizontal windows, ramp circulation, and modern residential planning. It is useful for understanding how structure, movement, and environmental ideas can be integrated in a building concept.

**2. Design studio knowledge**

Villa Savoye teaches how to turn a house into a sequence. Instead of treating rooms as isolated boxes, it organizes arrival, car movement, ramp, view, and roof terrace into one architectural promenade.

**3. Architectural history knowledge**

The building is a clear gateway into modernism and Le Corbusier's Five Points. More importantly, it shows how early modern architecture tried to redefine domestic life through technology, hygiene, mobility, and abstraction.

**4. What students often overlook**

Students often memorize the Five Points but forget to ask how the building is experienced. The ramp, the approach by car, the roof terrace, and the changing relationship to landscape are as important as the formal checklist.

**5. Further learning route**

Start with the Five Points, then trace the promenade through Villa Savoye. Next compare Unité d'Habitation to see how Le Corbusier moved from private villa to collective housing. Finally compare Farnsworth House and Row House in Sumiyoshi to test different ideas of modern domestic space.

## 3. Kimbell Art Museum

Architect: Louis Kahn
Current page slug: `kimbell-art-museum`

### Exam Connection

**1. Architect exam knowledge**

This case helps students understand museum planning, daylight control, roof structure, repeated structural bays, material durability, and the relationship between natural light and artwork. It is useful for studying how environmental control and architectural order shape public cultural buildings.

**2. Design studio knowledge**

Kimbell Art Museum teaches how to make a strong spatial system without visual noise. The repeated vaults, controlled light, and calm material palette show how structure can organize both movement and attention.

**3. Architectural history knowledge**

The building connects Louis Kahn's late modern monumentality with a renewed interest in material weight, timeless order, and institutional dignity. It helps students understand a path beyond glass-box modernism.

**4. What students often overlook**

Students may describe the museum as calm without identifying why. The calm comes from precise daylight modulation, structural repetition, gallery scale, and the way the building avoids both theatrical excess and neutral emptiness.

**5. Further learning route**

Study museum daylight strategies, then compare Kimbell with Salk Institute for Kahn's use of order and silence. After that, compare Kimbell with Louvre-Lens and Chichu Art Museum to understand different ways museums control attention.

## 4. Rolex Learning Center

Architect: SANAA
Current page slug: `rolex-learning-center`

### Exam Connection

**1. Architect exam knowledge**

This case helps students think about educational facilities, universal movement, open-plan organization, visual connection, zoning without conventional corridors, and the challenges of accessibility, orientation, and acoustic control in large continuous spaces.

**2. Design studio knowledge**

Rolex Learning Center teaches how plan can become landscape. It is valuable for students because it asks them to design relationships between learning, movement, meeting, solitude, and flexibility rather than simply arranging classrooms.

**3. Architectural history knowledge**

The building connects contemporary Japanese minimalism, post-institutional learning environments, and SANAA's interest in lightness, transparency, and non-hierarchical space. It helps students understand how contemporary architecture often replaces fixed rooms with fields of activity.

**4. What students often overlook**

Students often praise the openness but ignore the tradeoffs. An open learning landscape must still solve orientation, privacy, sound, accessibility, and furniture-based territorial boundaries.

**5. Further learning route**

Start by studying library and educational building organization. Then compare Rolex Learning Center with 21st Century Museum Kanazawa for non-hierarchical planning, and with Seattle Central Library for a more structured approach to public learning space.

## 5. 21st Century Museum Kanazawa

Architect: SANAA
Current page slug: `kanazawa-museum`

### Exam Connection

**1. Architect exam knowledge**

This case helps students understand museum circulation, public access, multiple entrances, transparent facade strategy, gallery zoning, and the relationship between cultural buildings and urban space. It is useful for thinking about how plan shape affects public behavior.

**2. Design studio knowledge**

21st Century Museum Kanazawa teaches how a building can weaken the idea of a single front. Students can study it as a model for public architecture that distributes access, reduces hierarchy, and turns circulation into civic openness.

**3. Architectural history knowledge**

The building connects contemporary museum design with SANAA's light, transparent, and non-monumental language. It shows a shift from museum as temple or palace toward museum as open public field.

**4. What students often overlook**

Students may focus on the circular form as an icon. The more important issue is institutional behavior: the circle, glass edge, and multiple entries change how visitors approach, enter, and understand the museum.

**5. Further learning route**

Study museum plans and public circulation first. Then compare Kanazawa with New Museum for horizontal versus vertical museum organization, Rolex Learning Center for field-like planning, and Kimbell Art Museum for a more ordered gallery sequence.

## Product Takeaway

These five examples suggest that Archistory can bridge three learning needs:

- For exam preparation, buildings become concrete examples of recurring architectural knowledge: light, circulation, structure, public access, educational planning, and museum organization.
- For design studio, buildings become prompts for judgment: what to emphasize, what to reduce, what tradeoff to accept, and how to defend a design decision.
- For architectural history, buildings become evidence of larger shifts: modernism, sacred modern space, late modern monumentality, and contemporary non-hierarchical planning.

The important product direction is not to turn Archistory into a code textbook. The value is to let a student open a building page and understand which architectural knowledge this case helps them unlock.
